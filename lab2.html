Frequently Asked Questions

A quick guide before you plan your trip.

The dry season, from November to May, is generally the best time to visit — especially for island destinations like Boracay, Siargao, and Palawan.

For popular spots like El Nido and Coron, booking a few days ahead during peak season (December–April) helps you avoid sold-out boat tours.

Sagada is roughly a 5-6 hour bus ride from Baguio, making the two a popular combined itinerary for mountain travelers.
